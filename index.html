The AI use it makes up for

Here, making up for AI use means your diet change saves as much carbon or water as your AI use costs. It does not undo local effects, such as strain on the grid or water supply near a data centre. The training share depends on how many prompts a model is assumed to serve, and the water figures for food are modelled with lower confidence.
